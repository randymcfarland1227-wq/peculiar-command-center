03/Brand studio

Archive, not a rebrand

The direction is botanical, archival, tactile, and slightly eccentric. The logo exists. Refine it. Do not replace it. Labels, packaging, and photography are still open.

Color

  • Deep forest

  • Olive

  • Sage

  • Cream

  • Stone

Direction, not a locked token file. Sage is a material color, not a button color.

Type

Peculiar

Display — expressive serif, old-world editorial.

Sizes, prices, care, batch codes.

Utility — clean sans for navigation and metadata.

Voice

Specific over green. The object should feel found. Sustainability explains the system; it does not open the sentence.

  • Museum shop, not rustic cabin
  • Cream wax, reclaimed glass, cork
  • Scent names from places and rooms

Decisions

  • Launch wax will likely be undyed.

    Fewer variables, lower cost, and the glass already supplies the color.

  • Six signature scents: Off the Record, Art Class Soap, Legend Has It, Haute Vanilla, Streetlights On, Butterfly Conservatory.

    Enough range to feel like a house, few enough to test properly.

  • Sustainability claims must be specific, not vague.

    The product has to be desirable before the circular story is explained.

  • Reclaimed vessel variation is a feature, not a defect.

    The draw is that Peculiar chooses the object.

  • Cork plus a beeswax detail is the leading closure.

    Fits the material palette. Not yet proven in heat or transit.

  • Desirability leads. Sustainability supports it.

    The buyer is paying for scent, object, and story — not the cheapest candle.

  • Labels are made in-house: home printer, sticker paper, cut on a Silhouette Cameo.

    No minimum order, and designs can change batch to batch.

  • The brand stays anonymous: no founder name or face.

    The brand's voice does the work a founder's face would. The mystery is part of the appeal.
