What is already settled

A working assumption can change when the revisit trigger hits. A decided item should not be relitigated because the checklist is long.

  • A planning range, not a price. Contribution target is about $20–25.

  • A burned or used candle cannot come back as a clean vessel credit.

  • The return credit has to be proven nearby before it becomes a system.

  • Fits the material palette. Not yet proven in heat or transit.

  • A single starting point so tests are comparable. Example: 10 oz wax × 6% = 0.6 oz fragrance.

  • Hot throw and consistency matter more than an oil-only rule. Essential oils stay where they improve a blend.

  • Enough range to feel like a house, few enough to test properly.

  • Keeps a slot for the lab without forcing it into the permanent line.

  • Plant-based positioning, a creamier look, and a better chance of fragrance performance.

  • Fewer variables, lower cost, and the glass already supplies the color.

  • Cure, QC, inventory release, and traceability need batches.

  • Safe, repeatable wicking cannot follow the shopper’s size label alone.

  • Three choices may be enough without turning the shop into a vessel catalog.

  • Bands leave room for reclaimed variation while staying shoppable.

  • Reclaimed carries the story. Recycled gives predictable inventory, simpler testing, and a reliable gift.

  • Peculiar chooses the vessel. The variation is the product.

  • The product has to be desirable before the circular story is explained.

  • The draw is that Peculiar chooses the object.

  • The buyer is paying for scent, object, and story — not the cheapest candle.

  • Direct first. Wholesale is out of scope for this version.
