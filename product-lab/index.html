Decisions in force

  • Peculiar will have both Reclaimed and Recycled collections.
  • Reclaimed customers choose size, scent, and vessel preference — not the exact vessel.
  • Working sizes are Small 8–10 oz, Medium 12–16 oz, and Large 17–20 oz.
  • Reclaimed vessels are also classified internally by diameter and profile.
  • Soy-coconut, or a soy-dominant coconut blend, is the leading wax.
  • Launch wax will likely be undyed.
  • Essential-oil-only is no longer required.
  • About five permanent signature scents are planned.
